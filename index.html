Chapter 04 · Ten days offline

Internet gaya.
Lekin hum nahi. ❤️

Ek time aaya jab tumhare town mein poore 10 din internet nahi tha. Main wahan 10 din raha — aur somehow woh 10 din humari story ke sabse memorable days ban gaye.

Hum cousins ke saath chhoti si trip par gaye, jungle mein tigers dekhe, main road par unhe bikers par attack karte hue witness kiya — aur us waqt car main drive kar raha tha, tum back seat par thi.

Bike wali bhabhi ke saath race, saath mein ice cream, garmi mein tumhare liye rumal lena… aur phir hours of ludo because internet hi nahi tha. 😄

One of those little moments I still remember.
A very important chapter 🎲

So we played Ludo.
A lot of Ludo.

Internet nahi tha, toh Ludo hi hamara internet ban gaya. Hours tak khelna, ek doosre ki goti kaatna, banter karna, hasna, phir rematch. 😂

Ye sirf ek game nahi tha. Those 10 offline days became our little world.
